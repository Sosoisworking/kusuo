Redesign

Kusuo, rethought

Four fixes carried through every screen: Reflect and Goals become tabs instead of Settings links; Today leads with the next habit and the shape of the week; Progress gets twelve weeks of signal instead of seven dots; and the 320px centred column becomes a flush-left, edge-to-edge layout.

Navigation

Today · Progress · Reflect · Goals in the tab bar. Settings moves to a gear in the Today header — it is a place you visit twice a year.

Today

A week strip for shape, one Next-up card for momentum, then the rest of the list. Opening the app always has an obvious single action.

Progress

A four-week heatmap of the whole practice, then per-habit consistency across twelve weeks. Streaks become one number among several, not the only reward.

Training — Push to Play Port

Kusuo gains a training module

Six tabs now: Today keeps the home slot with Reflect and Goals folded in as collapsed cards; Train, Splits, Calendar and Records come across from Push to Play; Settings takes the last slot with Account behind the profile menu. Calendar and Records carry a Habits / Training toggle. No rest timer, no trophies — records read as plain facts. Weight in kg with an lb toggle; a set records weight, reps and RPE.
