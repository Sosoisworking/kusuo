## Confirmed

- Kusuo was identified as a personal growth mobile app.
- Android + Kotlin were used.
- RoomDB was used for persistence.
- Package/application ID: `com.soso.kusuo`.
- Known code files included `MainActivity.kt`, `HomeActivity.kt`, `AddHabitActivity.kt`, `Habit.kt`, `HabitDao.kt`, and `HabitDatabase.kt`.
